## Details

1. **Player Gallery & Profile Hub.** A grid of original player art with filters. Each player opens a profile with bio, stats, contract and salary.
2. **Digital Trading Card Collection.** Player art becomes collectible cards with rarity tiers. Users open daily packs, build a collection and trade duplicates.
3. **Salary Cap & Trade Machine.** Propose a trade between two or three teams and check it against cap rules: salary matching, apron thresholds and roster limits.
4. **Value vs Salary Analytics Board.** Plots salary against performance to find who is overpaid and who is a bargain.
5. **Fantasy Roster Builder.** Draft a 10-player roster within a fake salary budget; rosters are compared using real stats.
6. **Live Game Companion.** Live scores, play-by-play and win probability, with alerts for close games.
7. **"Guess the Player" Daily Puzzle.** One mystery player a day, revealed through clues or a blurred crop of the art.
8. **Shot Chart Heatmap Visualiser.** Where a player shoots from and how efficiently, drawn on a court.
9. **Game Outcome Predictor.** An ML.NET model predicts upcoming games and tracks its own accuracy.
10. **NBA "What If" History Simulator.** Change one historical decision and simulate the seasons that follow.
